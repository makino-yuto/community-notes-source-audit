Figure 1. Current citation access and uncertainty in the same-post target population. (A) Machine census of 1,882 pairs: filled diamonds show any cited URL being machine-open; open circles show the expected open probability for one uniformly chosen distinct citation within each note. (B) Stratified AI-assisted browser-profile classifications of 240 pairs. Group segments show weighted lower and upper estimates when unresolved content is respectively unavailable or available. The paired difference has conservative unresolved-content bounds of -0.6 to +13.4 percentage points (thick segment), with a Bonferroni 95% outer design interval of -4.9 to +19.2 points (thin line). The thick segment is not a confidence interval. Bounds are conservative when the same unknown URL occurs in both notes. Intervals condition on AI classifications and exclude annotation error. Browser-profile access may depend on existing cookies and does not establish claim support. Neither panel estimates a causal authorship effect.
