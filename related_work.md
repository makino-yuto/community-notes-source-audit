# Related work and novelty boundary

Version: 2026-11-15. These are nearest primary sources found before collecting the study's URL outcomes. An absence in this short search is not proof of first discovery.

| Source | What it establishes | Boundary for this project |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [X Community Notes data documentation](https://github.com/twitter/communitynotes/blob/main/documentation/under-the-hood/download-data.md) | Official public Notes, Note Status History and User Enrollment tables; stable participant IDs; `apiEarnedIn` AI writers; daily snapshot/deletion behavior. | Data source and cohort definition, not a finding. Current enrollment is not a full historical log. |
| [X Community Writer documentation](https://github.com/twitter/communitynotes/blob/main/documentation/api/community-writer.md) and [technical report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40067) (Miller et al., 2026) | The official AI writer drafts, screens, revises and deletes notes; AI accounts supply a large share of Helpful notes. | Do not claim to discover AI note writing or deletion. Ask whether surviving Helpful notes retain independently retrievable evidence. |
| [AI Fact-Checking in the Wild](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.02592) (Li & Bakker, 2026) | Field comparison of LLM and human notes with attention to rating exposure and selection. | It evaluates helpfulness ratings, not later source-URL availability. Our AI/human comparison is descriptive and must control for note month. |
| [From Birdwatch to Community Notes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.09585) (Mohammadi et al., 2025) | Large curated historical dataset and extraction of embedded URLs. | URL extraction is prior work. This study must measure whether cited URLs can still be retrieved and distinguish true deletion from access barriers. |
| [References to unbiased sources increase helpfulness](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.10560) (2025) | External source links correlate with Community Note helpfulness. | Link presence and broad source categories are prior work; source durability is a different outcome. |
| [Detecting and Correcting Reference Hallucinations in Commercial LLMs and Deep Research Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03173) (2026) | URL health and Wayback checks for model-generated research citations; separates nonexistent from stale URLs. | URL-checking techniques are not novel. Transfer the measurement to deployed Community Notes and compare time-matched AI/human notes. |
| [Internet Archive Wayback CDX API](https://github.com/internetarchive/wayback/blob/master/wayback-cdx-server/README.md) | Capture timestamps, statuses and content digests can be queried. | A digest change is not semantic drift; use dated capture coverage and manual main-text checks. |
| [Community Notes AI-writer audit dashboard](https://alexmahadevan.github.io/cn-bot/ai-writers) (Mahadevan, 2026) | Public independent implementation already joins `apiEarnedIn` and counts deleted AI notes. | Writer classification and deletion counts alone are not a contribution; cite this precedent if used. |

**Potential contribution, conditional on results:** A reproducible, dated estimate of how often external sources attached to currently Helpful AI and human notes remain machine-readable, plus a deliberately small archived-content substudy and an audit protocol that separates true missing evidence from access barriers. The innovation claim must remain narrow. If source availability is high and cohort differences are small, the valid output is a benchmark and a null/limited finding, not a dramatic security claim.

**Publication caution:** The literature search did not find a dedicated paper on Community Notes source-link rot, but nearby work on general link rot, LLM citation health, and Community Notes URL extraction exists. Verify references and the novelty statement again before final submission.
